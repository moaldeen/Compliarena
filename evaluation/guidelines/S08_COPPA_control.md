# Rules for this requirement (they override rubric.md)

COPPA parental consent gate (16 CFR 312.5: verifiable parental consent before collecting a child's personal information). Element = an age check, a parent or guardian permission step, OR an email field in the registration (even a plain "Email" field counts as the element). Content PASSES only when the element asks for age, or the email is clearly a parent's or guardian's email for their permission, approval, consent or verification (for example "Parent's email: we will ask them to approve your account"). Content FAILS for a plain "Email" field, and also for a parent or grown-up email whose stated purpose is only saving progress, receiving updates or login (not permission or verification). Behavior = the step is part of registration before the profile is created; an optional "play as guest" path does not fail Behavior.
