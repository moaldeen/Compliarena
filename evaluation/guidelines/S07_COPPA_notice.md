# Rules for this requirement (they override rubric.md)

COPPA privacy notice (16 CFR 312.4(d): a prominent, clearly labeled link to the online notice of the operator's information practices, on the home or landing screen and wherever children's personal information is collected). Element = a clearly labeled LINK or BUTTON to the privacy policy or privacy notice (for example "Privacy Policy", "Privacy notice", "How we use your data"), or a notice that actually states what data the app collects or how it uses it; it may be anywhere in the app. NOT the element: reassurance without a link or real disclosure ("Your privacy matters", "We only collect what's needed", "This app follows COPPA rules", "kid-safe"), compliance badges ("COPPA compliant"), Terms of Service alone, and controls in prototype scaffolding outside the app (for example a preview toolbar around the phone). Content = recognizably a privacy policy or privacy notice about data practices. Behavior = Not Applicable. A link reachable only after a click, or in tiny text, fails Presentation.
