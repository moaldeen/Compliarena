# Rules for this requirement (they override rubric.md)

Account deletion. Element = an action that deletes the account (button or row), anywhere in the app. Generic "privacy", "data", or "manage your data" rows, sign out, or deactivate are NOT the element. Content = it clearly concerns deleting the whole account. Behavior = the user can start it in the app and gets a confirmation or cancel step. A row lower in the settings list is normal placement.
